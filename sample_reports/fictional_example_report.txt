Savanna Agro-Foods Plc Sustainability Report 2025

Message from the Chief Executive Officer
At Savanna, sustainability is in our DNA and we are proud to be the industry-leading green food company in West Africa. Our products are eco-friendly and good for the planet. We are committed to becoming carbon neutral and will continue on our journey towards a cleaner future for all Nigerians.

Climate and energy
In 2025 we installed 2.4 MW of rooftop solar at our Kano and Ibadan processing plants, which generated 3,350 MWh and replaced diesel generation. Our Scope 1 and 2 emissions fell by 18% from 41,200 tCO2e in our 2021 baseline year to 33,800 tCO2e in 2025, as verified by an independent third party under ISO 14064-3 with limited assurance. We target a 42% absolute reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030 against the 2021 baseline, a target validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi). We aim to reach net zero across our value chain by 2050. To compensate for our remaining emissions we purchase carbon credits from tree planting projects, which makes our flagship Sunrise cornflakes a carbon neutral product. Our entire supply chain is sustainable and we significantly reduce the environmental footprint of agriculture in Nigeria.

Water and packaging
Water withdrawal per tonne of product decreased by 12% compared with 2023, to 3.1 m3 per tonne. All of our packaging will be 100% recyclable. We strive to minimise plastic waste wherever possible and are working towards a circular economy. In 2025 we recovered and recycled 1,250 tonnes of post-consumer PET through our partnership with a certified recycler.

Nature and communities
Our farmers use natural and responsible methods that protect biodiversity. We planted 50,000 trees in partnership with local schools to promote environmental awareness. We seek to eliminate deforestation from our cocoa and soy supply chains, and from 2026 we will report deforestation-free volumes using satellite monitoring, with results assured by an external auditor.
